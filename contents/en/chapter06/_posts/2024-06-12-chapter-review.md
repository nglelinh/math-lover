## Discovery Puzzle

A snack costs 50,000 dong and is 20% off.

Before you compute, estimate: is the new price nearer 30,000, 40,000, or 50,000?

Then compute exactly.

Now add a decimal twist: you buy 1.5 of those snacks at the *new* price. What do you pay?

---

## Core Idea

### Trail 1: Decimals

Line up the points.

$$15.5 + 7.25 = 22.75$$

$$12.5 + 7.8 = 20.3$$

$$9.5 - 4.25 = 5.25$$

Slide the point for 10, 100, and 1000. Count decimal places when you multiply.

### Trail 2: Ratios and percent

If 4 notebooks cost 20,000 dong, the unit rate is

$$20000 \div 4 = 5000$$

7 notebooks cost

$$5000 \times 7 = 35000$$

That is direct proportion: more books, more money, same price each.

A 20% sale on 50,000 dong:

Discount $$50000 \times 20/100 = 10000$$

New price $$40000$$ dong

### Trail 3: Choosing a tool

Ask three questions:

- Are the numbers wearing decimal points? Line them up or slide them.
- Are two quantities keeping the same relationship? Use a ratio or a unit rate.
- Is the story “out of 100”? Use percent.

## Guided Discovery Activities

### Activity 1: Mixed warm-up

1. Compute $$12.6 + 8.35$$.
2. A 50,000 dong item is 20% off. New price?
3. 3 bottles cost 27,000 dong. What do 5 bottles cost at the same unit price?

Answers to check later: $$20.95$$; $$40000$$; $$45000$$.

### Activity 2: Life stories (number language only)

1. 5 kg of apples cost 50,000 dong. What do 8 kg cost?
2. A shirt is 200,000 dong, 15% off. What do you pay?

Leave clock-reading and unit-converting puzzles for Chapter 11. Here we only need rates and percents.

## Example Walkthrough

$$12.5 + 7.8 = 20.3$$

$$9.5 - 4.25$$: write $$9.50 - 4.25 = 5.25$$

5 kg for 50,000 → 1 kg for 10,000 → 8 kg for 80,000.

200,000 dong, 15% off → discount 30,000 → pay 170,000.

If 3 notebooks cost 18,000, then 1 costs 6,000 and 5 cost 30,000. Same skeleton as the apple story.
