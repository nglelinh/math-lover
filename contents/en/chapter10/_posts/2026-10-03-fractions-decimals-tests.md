Almost every grade-6 entrance paper has **fractions or decimals**.

---

## Objectives

- compare and order fractions
- convert fraction ↔ decimal when useful
- solve “a fraction of …” word problems

---

## Core Idea

Order: $$\dfrac{{7}}{{12}} < \dfrac{{2}}{{3}} < \dfrac{{3}}{{4}} < \dfrac{{5}}{{6}}$$ (common denominator or benchmarks).

Word problem model: “$$\dfrac{{3}}{{5}}$$ of 360 balls are blue” → blue $$216$$, yellow $$144$$.

---

## Practice


1. Order $$\dfrac{{5}}{{8}},\dfrac{{11}}{{12}},\dfrac{{7}}{{8}},\dfrac{{9}}{{10}}$$.  
2. Convert $$0.36$$ to a simplified fraction.

---

## Exercises

1. Compare $$\dfrac{{5}}{{6}}$$ and $$\dfrac{{7}}{{8}}$$.  
2. Three numbers sum to $$2022$$; first is $$\dfrac{{1}}{{3}}$$ of second; third is double the first. Largest number?  
3. Unit conversion: area units mixed $$\mathrm{{dm}}^2$$ and $$\mathrm{{cm}}^2$$.
