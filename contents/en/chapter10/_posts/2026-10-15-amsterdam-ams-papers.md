## Objectives

- use AMS as a thinking gym
- spot hard pattern / folding / multi-step items
- skip smartly when stuck

---

## Core Idea

Longer creative items: sequences, paper folding nets, percent chains, shaded areas, circular tracks.

---

## Practice


1. Three short AMS fill-ins untimed.  
2. Outline a hard item in 4 steps.

---

## Exercises

Select 2023–2024 fill-ins; one shaded area; one circular motion.

---

## Parent Corner

Treat AMS as enrichment, not the admission ticket.
