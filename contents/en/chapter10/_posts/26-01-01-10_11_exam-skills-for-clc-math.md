Knowing topics is not enough — you need **exam strategy**.

---

## Objectives

- budget time by paper structure
- use a 3-pass order
- check answers with estimation

---

## Core Idea

Pass 1 easy → pass 2 medium → pass 3 hard.  
Never spend 15 minutes stuck on one item early.

---

## Practice


1. 20-minute speed round on easy items.  
2. Write 3 recurring errors in a log.

---

## Exercises

1. Weekly plan: 3 topic sessions + 1 timed paper.  
2. One full 45-minute mock.  
3. Five-item pre-exam checklist.

---

## Parent Corner

Label every sheet with **school + year**. Fix repeated mistakes before starting new papers.
