**Logic and unknowns** appear in TDN/Thu Duc and Hanoi equation items.

---

## Objectives

- set variables and conditions
- solve tiny linear systems
- count constrained placements (link to ch.09)

---

## Core Idea

$$3A=30\Rightarrow A=10$$; then split $$B+C$$ and $$B-C$$.

3 tokens on a $$3\times 3$$ board with one per row/column → permutations.

---

## Practice


1. Solve the A,B,C system.  
2. Count 3-token placements.

---

## Exercises

1. Solve the multi-term equation for $$x$$.  
2. Build a 3×3 grid with row sum 12.
