## Objectives

- spot arithmetic / growing-difference patterns
- find the $$n$$th term
- read visual patterns (cans, dots)

---

## Core Idea

$$3;4;6;9;13$$ has differences $$+1,+2,+3,+4$$.

Visual cans (1; 7; 19) need a formula in the box index.

---

## Practice


1. Next terms of $$2;5;10;17$$.  
2. Mean of $$15;45;75$$.

---

## Exercises

1. 20th term of $$3;4;6;9;13;\ldots$$  
2. Predict can-box 5 and 10 with a rule.  
3. Digit-counting on odd house numbers 1..19.
