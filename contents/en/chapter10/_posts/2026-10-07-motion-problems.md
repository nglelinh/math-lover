**Motion problems** reward a clean $$s=v\times t$$ model.

---

## Objectives

- master $$s=v t$$ and unit changes
- model same direction vs opposite
- handle late/early variants

---

## Core Idea

Opposite meeting speed: $$v_1+v_2$$.  
Late/early: one distance, two speeds → time difference.

---

## Practice


1. 70 km uses 10 L fuel → 280 km uses?  
2. Convert 15 minutes to hours.

---

## Exercises

1. $$v=40$$ km/h, $$t=2.5$$ h → $$s$$.  
2. Late 30 min at 30 km/h; early 15 min at 40 km/h — find fair speed.  
3. 1.2 km in 6 min → km/h.

---
