**Age problems** are staples of Hanoi CLC papers.

---

## Objectives

- choose unknowns for present ages
- encode “older by”, “times as old”, “in $$n$$ years”
- check by substituting back

---

## Core Idea

Child $$x$$, mother older by 36 and 4 times as old: $$x+36=4x\Rightarrow x=12$$.

Age **difference stays constant** over time.

---

## Practice


1. Siblings sum to 20; older by 4 — younger age?  
2. Draw a timeline for “in 5 years”.

---

## Exercises

1. Mother older by 36 and 4× child. Child’s age?  
2. Invent one age problem for a partner.
