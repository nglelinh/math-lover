## Objectives

- use divisibility rules
- solve multi-condition two-digit number problems
- apply inclusion–exclusion for counting multiples

---

## Core Idea

In 1..100, multiples of 3: $$33$$.  
Turn off multiples of 3 then of 5 → inclusion–exclusion for how many stay on.

---

## Practice


1. Two-digit numbers divisible by 7 with digit sum 6.  
2. Count multiples of 2 or 3 in 1..20.
