CLC exams often open with **“Compute”** or **“compute smartly”**.

---

## Objectives

- follow order of operations
- use grouping (distributive property)
- avoid decimal traps

---

## Core Idea

Parentheses → multiply/divide → add/subtract.

Example: $$2.75 \times 0.99 + 2.75 : 100 = 2.75 \times 1 = 2.75$$

---

## Practice


1. Compute $$18\times 5 + 18\times 5$$ two ways.  
2. Fix: $$3+4\times 2$$ is **not** 14.
