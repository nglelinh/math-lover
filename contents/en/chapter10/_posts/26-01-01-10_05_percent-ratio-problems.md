**Percent and ratio** dominate word problems in CLC tests.

---

## Objectives

- convert % ↔ fraction ↔ decimal
- solve increase/decrease chains
- use ratios in geometry stories

---

## Core Idea

After a 15% discount, pay 212,500 → original $$212500/0.85$$.

Increase 10% then decrease 10% does **not** return to the start.

---

## Practice


1. 10% off of 4000.  
2. +12%, then +10%, then −10% vs original.

---

## Exercises

1. Increase 150 by 20%.  
2. Library 1000 books, +10% per year for 2 years.  
3. In triangle, $$AM:MC=2:1$$ — fraction of area for $$BMC$$.
