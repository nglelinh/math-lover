**Selective and specialized** middle schools often use **math entrance tests** (and logic) for grade 6.

This chapter organizes **real exam patterns** into **topics** and **school-focused lessons**.

---

## Objectives

- know which topics appear most in CLC tests
- plan study time before the exam
- connect each pattern to earlier course chapters

---

## Core Idea

| Track | Examples | Style |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Hanoi CLC** | NTT, Marie Curie, LTV, Cau Giay | Written / mixed, 45–90 min |
| **HCM** | Tran Dai Nghia, Thu Duc cluster | Math + logic in a larger paper |

**Topic map:** computation → word problems → geometry → sequences/logic → exam skills → school papers.

---

## Practice


1. Circle your **3 weakest** topics.  
2. Pick **target school + backup** with a parent.  
3. Schedule **one timed paper** this week.

---

## Exercises

1. List 5 exam topics you will review first.  
2. Start an error log: **type | mistake | lesson to relearn**.  
3. Choose one 45-minute practice date on the calendar.

---

## Parent Corner

Steady practice beats cramming. Use chapters 01–09 when the foundation is weak; use chapter 10 for **exam patterns**.
