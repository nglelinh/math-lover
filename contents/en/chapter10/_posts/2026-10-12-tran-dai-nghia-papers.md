**Tran Dai Nghia (TDN)** is a flagship HCMC grade-6 entrance track. Math + logic is part of the written paper.


![TDN 2024 — page 1]({{ site.baseurl }}/img/chapter10/tdn-2024-trang-01.png)

*TDN 2024 — page 1*


---

## Objectives

- know the usual 3-item TDN math–logic structure
- practice patterns, counting/logic, and geometry–fraction mixes

---

## Core Idea

2024 lanterns (divisibility), 2025 cans + 3 tokens on $$3\times 3$$, 2026 odd house numbers + transfers.

---

## Practice


1. Multiples of 3 in 1..100.  
2. List 6 placements of 3 tokens.

---

## Exercises

Timed 2024 set; 2025 cans; 2026 digit costs.
