## Objectives

- know MCQ + short + long structure
- dodge decimal/% traps in MCQs
- write clear two-part solutions

---

## Core Idea

Classic 2023 openers: smart product, fraction of balls, tank water level, chart percent, then motion + geometry.

---

## Practice


1. Compute the sample expression.  
2. Tag each item A–D topic.

## Exercises

2023 Section I–II; one full timed paper.
