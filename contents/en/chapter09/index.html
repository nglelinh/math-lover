This chapter teaches systematic counting when there are many choices: either-or decisions, multi-step choices, ordered arrangements, and unordered selections.

Lessons use games, objects, and tree diagrams — no heavy formulas — for ages 8–10, building naturally on probability ideas from the previous chapter.

Optional enrichment: character creators and password length are counting problems in disguise.
