Three friends **An, Bo, Chi** line up for a photo. **ABC** is different from **ACB** — order matters!

We are counting **permutations** (ordered arrangements).

---

## Objectives

- tell when order changes the outcome
- list or multiply decreasing choices: $$3 \times 2 \times 1 = 6$$
- contrast with unordered groups (next lesson)

---

## Core Idea

Line up 3 people:

- 3 choices for first spot
- 2 for second
- 1 left for third

$$3 \times 2 \times 1 = 6$$ orders.

Four people: $$4 \times 3 \times 2 \times 1 = 24$$.

---

## Exercises

1. Arrange 3 toys on a shelf. How many orders?
2. Four friends in a line. How many ways?
3. Two-digit numbers using 1,2,3,4 without repeating digits. How many?
