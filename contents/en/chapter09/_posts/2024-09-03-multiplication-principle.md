Pick **one shirt**, **then** pick **one pair of pants**. Every shirt works with every pair.

This is the **multiplication principle**: for sequential steps, **multiply** the number of choices at each step.

---

## Objectives

- recognize multi-step “and / then” problems
- multiply counts and check with a table
- extend to three or more steps

---

## Core Idea

$$3 \text{ shirts} \times 2 \text{ pants} = 6 \text{ outfits}$$

Three steps: 2 hats × 3 shirts × 2 shoes:

$$2 \times 3 \times 2 = 12$$

---

## Exercises

1. 5 snacks × 2 drinks = ? pairs.
2. Flip a coin 3 times. How many result strings?
3. 3 shirts, 3 pants, 2 hats — one of each. How many looks?
