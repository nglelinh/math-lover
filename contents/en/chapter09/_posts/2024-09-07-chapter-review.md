You finished **Combinatorics and Counting**! Review the four big tools:

| Situation | Keyword | Tool |
|-----------|---------|------|
| Organized list | no misses | table / list |
| One of several groups | **or** | **add** |
| Several steps in a row | **and / then** | **multiply** |
| Order matters | lineup | decreasing multiply |
| Order ignored | pick a team | list pairs |
| Many branches | choices | **tree diagram** |
