When choices branch in several steps, a long list gets messy. A **tree diagram** shows every path from start to finish.

---

## Objectives

- draw trees for 2–3 step problems
- count end branches (= outcomes)
- connect trees to add vs multiply

---

## Core Idea

2 shirts, 2 pants → tree splits 2 then 2 → **4** leaves (= $$2 \times 2$$).

**OR** choices: bus (2 routes) or bike (3 routes) → separate branches, **add**: $$2 + 3 = 5$$.

---

## Exercises

1. Draw a tree: 2 shirt colors, 3 pant colors. Count leaves.
2. A→B (2 roads) then B→C (3 roads). Draw and count paths.
3. Match leaf count to multiply or add.
