You open a closet with **3 shirts** and **2 pairs of pants**. How many different outfits are there? If you count randomly, you might skip one or count the same outfit twice.

Today we learn **systematic counting**: list every possibility in an organized way.

![Dice — each face is one outcome](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/8/82/Dice.svg/960px-Dice.svg.png)

*Image: Wikimedia Commons*

---

## Objectives

By the end of this lesson, you can:

- count outcomes without missing any
- avoid counting the same result twice
- use tables or lists to check your work

---

## Core Idea

**Golden rule:** pick a fixed order — for example **shirt first, then pants**.

| Shirt | Pants | Outfit |
|-------|-------|--------|
| Red | Black | Red–Black |
| Red | White | Red–White |
| Blue | Black | Blue–Black |
| Blue | White | Blue–White |

Total: **4 outfits**.

---

## Practice

1. Two front doors (A, B) and two back doors (1, 2). List every **front + back** pair → **4** ways.
2. Cards **M** and **P** — how many 2-letter words using each card once? **MP, PM** → **2** words.

---

## Exercises

1. Three pen colors: red, blue, yellow. Pick exactly one. How many choices?
2. Match 2 breads with 3 drinks in a table. How many pairs?
3. Flip a coin twice (H/T). List all results. How many?
