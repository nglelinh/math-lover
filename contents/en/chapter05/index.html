This learning track focuses on integers, square numbers, factors, multiples, primes, greatest common factors, and least common multiples.

It helps children see the structure of whole numbers — and the relationships between them — before they move on to decimals, measurement, and applications.

Optional enrichment: primes and shared multiples quietly power number locks and repeating game events.
